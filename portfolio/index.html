01. About me

I'm a computer engineering student who ended up loving the software side more than anything else. Somewhere between a logic gate and a for-loop, I picked a side.

Most of what I know came from curiosity rather than a syllabus: opening the editor at midnight, breaking something, fixing it, and learning why it broke. I like clean code, small tools that do one thing well, and understanding what's happening a layer below where I'm working.

Right now I'm in a summer training internship, sharpening the fundamentals and shipping more than I read about shipping.
